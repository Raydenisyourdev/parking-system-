ALGORITHM: Complete Parking System
1. START
2. Admin logs into the system.
3. IF login is correct, open dashboard.
4. Admin enters vehicle plate number.
5. Admin selects an available parking space.
6. Validate the plate and space.
7. IF validation fails, display error and return to dashboard.
8. Generate QR token.
9. Save vehicle in database.
10. Change space to RESERVED.
11. Driver scans QR code.
12. IF QR is valid:
       Record entry time.
       Change vehicle to ENTERING.
       Start car animation.
    ELSE:
       Reject scan.
13. After animation:
       Change vehicle to PARKED.
       Change space to OCCUPIED.
14. Driver remains parked.
15. Driver scans QR again when leaving.
16. IF vehicle is PARKED:
       Record exit time.
       Change vehicle to EXITING.
    ELSE:
       Reject exit.
17. Calculate total parking time.
18. Subtract grace period.
19. Calculate fee:
       0 - 30 minutes = KSh 0
       Over 30 min - 2 hours = KSh 50
       Over 2 - 4 hours = KSh 100
       Over 4 - 6 hours = KSh 300
       Over 6 hours = KSh 500
20. Display amount and payment methods.
21. Record payment.
22. IF payment is successful:
       Change vehicle to COMPLETED.
       Change space to AVAILABLE.
       Update revenue.
       Record activity.
23. Update dashboard.
24. END
