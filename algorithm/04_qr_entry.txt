ALGORITHM: QR Entry
1. START
2. Driver scans QR code.
3. Read the QR token.
4. Find the vehicle linked to the token.
5. Check if the token is valid.
6. IF invalid:
      Reject the scan.
      STOP.
7. Check the vehicle status.
8. IF the vehicle can enter:
      Record entry time.
      Change status to ENTERING.
      Start entry process.
   ELSE:
      Reject entry.
9. END
