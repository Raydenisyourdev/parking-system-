ALGORITHM: Payment Calculation
1. START
2. Get billable parking time.
3. Convert the time to minutes and hours.
4. IF time <= 30 minutes:
      Amount = KSh 0
5. ELSE IF time <= 2 hours:
      Amount = KSh 50
6. ELSE IF time <= 4 hours:
      Amount = KSh 100
7. ELSE IF time <= 6 hours:
      Amount = KSh 300
8. ELSE:
      Amount = KSh 500
9. Return amount.
10. END

PAYMENT RULES:
0 - 30 minutes = KSh 0
Over 30 min - 2 hours = KSh 50
Over 2 - 4 hours = KSh 100
Over 4 - 6 hours = KSh 300
Over 6 hours = KSh 500
