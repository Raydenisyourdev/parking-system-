ALGORITHM: QR Exit
1. START
2. Driver scans QR code.
3. Find the vehicle using the QR token.
4. Check whether the vehicle exists.
5. Check whether vehicle status is PARKED.
6. IF not PARKED:
      Reject exit.
      STOP.
7. Record EXIT_TIME.
8. Change status from PARKED to EXITING.
9. Calculate parking duration.
10. Calculate parking fee.
11. Display payment information.
12. END
