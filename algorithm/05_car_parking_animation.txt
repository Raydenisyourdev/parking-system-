ALGORITHM: Car Parking Animation
1. START
2. Get vehicle ID and assigned parking space.
3. Find the vehicle on the interface.
4. Find the assigned parking space.
5. Animate the car moving into the space.
6. Wait until the animation finishes.
7. Confirm the vehicle has parked.
8. Change vehicle status from ENTERING to PARKED.
9. Change space status from RESERVED to OCCUPIED.
10. END
